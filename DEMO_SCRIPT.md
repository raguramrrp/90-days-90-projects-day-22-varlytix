# SIGMORA Interactive Demo Script

Use this structured walkthrough for product presentations, live demos, or portfolio reviews.

---

## Act 1: Instant Clarity — Health Score & Plain-English Intelligence (1.5 mins)
1. Launch SIGMORA at `http://localhost:8501`.
2. Notice the clean, editorial research workstation design (no generic dark terminal, no noisy crypto widgets).
3. Select **Balanced Growth Portfolio** from the global header.
4. Highlight the **Portfolio Health Score (0–100)**:
   - Institutional Grade (e.g. 84/100, Grade A).
   - 4-Pillar breakdown: Diversification, Downside Resilience, Sharpe Efficiency, Tail-Risk Safety.
5. Review the **3-Bullet Plain-English Executive Summary**:
   - **The Strength:** Clear explanation of risk-adjusted productivity.
   - **The Blindspot:** Practical heads-up on concentration or equity co-movement.
   - **The Stress Reality:** Actual rupee impact in a severe market correction (e.g. ₹ loss).
6. Expand the **Investor Risk Appetite Profiler**:
   - Take the 4-question behavioral quiz and see instant feedback contrasting stated psychology vs actual portfolio volatility.

---

## Act 2: One-Click Broker Statement Import (1 min)
1. Click the **Portfolio** tab.
2. Select the **One-Click Broker Import** tab.
3. Click **Load Zerodha Kite CSV** (or Groww, Angel One, Upstox).
4. Point out the instantaneous schema detection:
   - Auto-detects columns: Instrument, Qty, Avg Cost.
   - Displays live parsed preview with Indian equities (Reliance, TCS, HDFC, GoldBeES).
5. Explain how investors can import their actual statements or test immediately with 1 click.

---

## Act 3: Multi-Asset Hedging & Correlation (1.5 mins)
1. Switch portfolio to **All-Weather Multi-Asset Portfolio** (combines Equities, GoldBeES, and LiquidBeES).
2. Go to the **Correlation** tab:
   - Notice that GoldBeES exhibits virtually zero or negative correlation ($r \approx 0.02$) to equities, acting as a genuine shock absorber.
   - LiquidBeES acts as a steady cash yield buffer.
3. Click the **Risk Lab** tab:
   - Show how Euler component risk decomposition reveals that Gold and Debt contribute negligible risk while expanding portfolio resilience.

---

## Act 4: Monte Carlo with Monthly SIP & Financial Goal Probability (2 mins)
1. Click the **Simulation** tab.
2. Input a **Monthly SIP Inflow (₹ 25,000)** and a **Financial Goal Target (₹ 50,00,000)**.
3. Select **1-Year or 3-Year horizon** and click **Execute Monte Carlo Simulation**.
4. Examine the **Fan Chart**:
   - Amber dotted line for the **Goal Target**.
   - Gray dashed baseline for **Total Invested Capital (Starting + SIPs)**.
   - Deep Teal core trajectory.
   - Highlight the calculated **Goal Success Probability (e.g. 78.4%)** and **Probability of Capital Loss**.

---

## Act 5: Interactive "What If?" Rebalancing Sandbox (2 mins)
1. Click the **Optimizer** tab.
2. Select the **Interactive 'What If?' Rebalancing Sandbox** tab.
3. Show the live sliders for every position.
4. Move the **GoldBeES** slider up to 15% and trim an aggressive stock:
   - Notice the **Real-Time Impact Table** instantly updating:
     - Volatility drops ($\Delta -3.8\%$).
     - Sharpe increases ($\Delta +0.25$).
     - Estimated max drawdown reduces.
   - Interactive horizontal bar chart dynamically compares Current vs What-If allocation.

---

## Act 6: Stress Testing & PDF Reporting (1.5 mins)
1. Click the **Stress Test** tab to simulate uniform crashes (-15%, -25%) and sector shocks.
2. Click the **Reports** tab to generate a publication-grade, multi-page PDF executive dossier.
   - Minimum Variance Allocation
   - Maximum Sharpe Allocation
3. Click the **Reports** tab.
4. Click **Generate & Download PDF Report** to produce an executive-ready multi-page dossier powered by ReportLab.
5. Conclude with the transparent financial disclaimer: *Hypothetical statistical scenarios designed to explore uncertainty, not investment advice.*
